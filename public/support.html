Getting started

  1. Sign in or create an account (needed to create a game; anyone can watch).
  2. Tap + → New Tournament. Set the buy-in, bounty and blind levels, add players, choose an admin PIN and tap Start Tournament.
  3. Share the room code or link. Everyone at the table follows the same clock on their phone or in a browser.
  4. Record knockouts as they happen, then Finish Tournament to set the payouts and see the results.

Common questions

  • How do I join a game? On Home, tap + → Join with Code and enter the 5-character room code, or open the link someone shared.
  • How do I take over as admin on another phone? Open the room's Room tab, enter the admin PIN and tap Unlock Admin.
  • I'm not getting level alerts. Check Settings → Alerts in the app, and allow notifications for Home Poker Tracker in the iPhone's Settings. With the phone locked, alerts arrive as notifications and on the Lock Screen.
  • I lost my connection during a game. Keep playing: changes are saved on the phone and sync when you're back online.
  • Where do old games go? Games with no activity for 30 days are removed. The iPhone app keeps a copy of finished games on the phone.

Account and privacy

  • Reset your password: on the sign-in screen, tap Forgot Password?.
  • Delete your account: in the iPhone app, Settings → Account → Delete Account, or email us.
  • See the Privacy Policy for what data is used and who can see it.

About

Home Poker Tracker is a scorekeeping tool for private games among friends. It doesn't take bets, hold money or move money. Developed by KSP.
