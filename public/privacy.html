♠ Privacy Policy

Home Poker Tracker — iPhone app and web app (poker-tracker-2ab9c.web.app)
Effective 9 November 2026

Home Poker Tracker helps a group of friends run a home poker tournament: the blind clock, knockouts, payouts and settling up, plus groups and "Who's in?" polls to organise games. This policy explains what data the iPhone app and the web app use, who can see it, and how to remove it. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Who is responsible

Home Poker Tracker is developed and operated by KSP. Contact: sai.koppolu@gmail.com.

Data we use

  • Your account (only if you sign in or register): email address, display name, and a user ID. Your password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see or store it.
  • Your profile (optional): a profile picture you choose (cropped and shrunk to a small square image) and a short status line.
  • Groups: a group's name and its members (display names of members with an account, and names typed in for people without one), and who its admins are.
  • Polls: a poll's title and start time, who posted it, and each member's answer (In, Buy-in or Out) with their name.
  • Notification registration (only if you allow notifications): a push token that lets us deliver notifications to your phone or browser, linked to your account.
  • Game data: the room name, players' names, buy-ins, bounties, knockouts, payouts, the blind structure and a log of actions (for example "Ann recorded a knockout"), with the name of who did each action.
  • Finished games: a short history entry (title, date, winner, number of players, the players' names, and the accounts of who created and took part in the game).
  • Player tones (optional): a short sound you upload to play when the blinds go up, with your name.
  • Settings on your device: appearance, alert preferences, recently opened rooms and similar preferences, stored only on your phone or in your browser. The iPhone app also keeps a copy of finished games on the phone.

We don't collect location, contacts, advertising identifiers or usage analytics, and we only access your photos when you pick a profile picture yourself.

Who can see it

  • Game rooms can be read by anyone who has the room code or link, and running games appear in the "Ongoing games" list. Don't put anything in a room name or player name that you wouldn't share with the table.
  • Player profiles (display name, profile picture and status) and game history can be read by other signed-in users, so registered players can be found and added to groups and games. The apps show you your own games and your groups' games. Your email address is only used for signing in and is never shown to other players.
  • Groups and polls can only be read by the group's members. When you share a poll (for example in WhatsApp), the message includes its title and start time; the people you share it with need to be signed-in members of the group to open the poll itself.
  • Uploaded tones can be played by anyone using the app.

Splitwise (optional)

If you connect Splitwise to record a game's results, you sign in on Splitwise's own page. Our Splitwise connector (a small service hosted on Cloudflare) receives a Splitwise access token, which is stored encrypted on your device and is only used to read your Splitwise group's members and to create the game's expense in that group. The expense contains the players' names and amounts. Splitwise's own privacy policy applies to data in Splitwise.

Notifications

Level-change and knockout alerts are local notifications created on your device.

If you allow notifications, we also send push notifications about new polls in your groups, games starting, poll reminders and being added to a group. They are sent by our connector on Cloudflare through Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (and, on iPhone, Apple's push notification service). A notification contains the poll, game or group name and the name of who posted it. You can turn notifications off at any time in your phone's or browser's settings; signing out stops them on that device.

Service providers

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Realtime Database in the EU region europe-west1, Hosting) stores the account and game data.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple Push Notification service deliver push notifications, if you allow them.
  • Cloudflare runs our connector for Splitwise and for sending notifications.
  • Splitwise, only if you choose to connect it.

How long we keep data

  • Games with no activity for 30 days are deleted automatically, together with their history entry.
  • Your account, profile, picture and status are kept until you change or delete them.
  • Groups are kept until they're deleted; polls disappear from the apps 12 hours after their start time or once their game starts.
  • A notification registration is removed when you sign out on that device, delete your account, or the device is no longer reachable.
  • Settings and saved games on your device stay until you delete the app or clear the browser's site data.

Deleting your account

In the iPhone app, go to Settings → Profile → Delete Account. This deletes your sign-in, your player profile, your profile picture and status, and your notification registrations. Games you played remain in their rooms and history for the other players until they're removed after 30 days of inactivity, and you stay listed in groups until you leave them or an admin removes you. You can also email us to delete your account or to ask for your name to be removed from a game.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU GDPR), you can ask to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, and object to or restrict its processing. Email us and we'll respond within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority. We process account and game data because it's needed to provide the service you asked for.

Children

Home Poker Tracker is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and is meant for adults organising private games. We don't knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will be mentioned in the app.
